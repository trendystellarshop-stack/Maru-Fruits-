### Blog Commenting Strategy:

**Criteria for Blog Selection:**
- ✅ Relevant to food, fruits, or health
- ✅ Active blog with engaged audience
- ✅ Dofollow backlinks (when possible)
- ✅ Good domain authority (DA 20+)
- ✅ Comments moderated (quality over spam)

**Comment Guidelines Followed:**
1. **Meaningful feedback** - Always add value to discussion
2. **Natural language** - No keyword stuffing
3. **Relevant to article** - Comments relate to post content
4. **Professional tone** - Maintain business reputation
5. **No spam** - No excessive promotion
6. **Website link included** - When allowed
7. **Varied anchor text** - Not always exact match
